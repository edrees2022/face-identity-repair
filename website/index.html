Research Scope & Headline

We empirically examine how target and co-present protected identities change under existing native UniPortrait conditioning regimes. Across 119 accepted two-person baseline scenes, Study 1 measures continuous identity-similarity changes under target-only single-ID generation versus an unconditioned comparator; Study 2 uses matched output seeds to evaluate single-ID versus dual-ID regimes. Results characterize the evaluated implementation rather than introducing a new generation algorithm or isolated mechanism ablation.

Scientific Boundaries & Disclosures

  • Evaluated Architecture: Scope is bounded to native UniPortrait with Stable Diffusion v1.5 in two-person compositions.
  • Complete Native Regimes: A1 and A2 represent distinct intact operational regimes; this study does not claim an isolated component-level causal ablation.
  • Continuous Identity Similarity: Positive paired contrast denotes relative attenuation of identity loss rather than guaranteed absolute restoration.
  • Scope Boundary: Paper A is strictly an empirical study of existing native regimes. It does not introduce a new generation algorithm or selective intervention policy (future Paper B).
