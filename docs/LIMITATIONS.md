# Scientific Boundaries & Limitations

1. **Evaluated Implementation:** Findings characterize UniPortrait with SD 1.5 in two-person compositions.
2. **Native Regimes:** A1 and A2 are compared as intact native regimes; this study is not an isolated component-level causal ablation.
3. **Continuous Metric:** Continuous cosine similarity differences do not imply binary identity preservation.
4. **Out-of-Scope:** Future Paper B predictive risk models and selective intervention policies are outside the scope of Paper A.
